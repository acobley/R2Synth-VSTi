R2Synth

A monophonic analogue-style synthesiser: three VCOs (sawtooth or square), an MS-20-style LFO whose shape is swept by a second LFO, a switchable filter (Moog ladder, MS-20 early or MS-20 late), two ADSR envelopes and portamento. A VST3 and an Audio Unit.

This installs R2Synth for every user on this Mac:

  • VST3 — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

You choose which on the next-but-one pane. Installing over an existing copy replaces it; your projects and their settings are untouched.

Quit your DAW before installing. A host that is already running holds the old plug-in open and will not see the new one until it is restarted.
