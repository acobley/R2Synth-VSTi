Installed

Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins if it does not do that by itself. R2Synth appears as an instrument by AE Cobley.

Worth knowing:

  • Everything works from the start. After a 7-day evaluation R2Synth shows a reminder now and then. A free activation code stops it: click the Evaluation line at the top of the panel, then Get activation code.
  • It is monophonic, last note wins, and every new note retriggers both envelopes — as on the MS-20.
  • Velocity is ignored, like the MS-20’s keyboard.
